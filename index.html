About

I'm a product and interaction designer who cares about the details that make software feel effortless. I partner closely with product managers, engineers and researchers to frame the right problem, explore widely, and ship the simplest solution that moves the metric.

Accessibility isn't a checklist item for me. It's how I design. Every project I lead meets WCAG 2.2 AA, and I've helped teams build that into their systems and review processes.
